## Part 1: Granting Access to Google Search Console (GSC)

Google Search Console allows our team to monitor indexation, track AI answer engine grounding, submit sitemaps, and diagnose technical search issues.

### Step 1: Open Search Console Settings
1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) and log into the account managing your domain property.
2. Select your website domain property from the top-left dropdown menu.
3. In the left sidebar navigation, scroll down and click on **Settings** (gear icon).

![[Placeholder Screenshot: Google Search Console Settings & Users]](/images/docs/aeo-access/gsc-users-permissions-placeholder.svg)

### Step 2: Open Users and Permissions
1. Under the **General settings** section, click on **Users and permissions**.
2. In the top-right corner of the Users table, click the blue **Add User** button.

### Step 3: Add Uprise Digital as an Owner/Full Access User
1. In the **Email address** field, enter:
   **`ned@uprisedigital.com.au`**
2. In the **Permission** dropdown menu, select **Full** (or **Owner** if schema/sitemap authorization requires full ownership).
3. Click **Add** to confirm.

![[Placeholder Screenshot: GSC Add User Modal]](/images/docs/aeo-access/gsc-add-user-modal-placeholder.svg)

## Part 2: Granting Website CMS Administrator Access

To implement AEO structured data (JSON-LD), entity markup, content updates, and site speed optimizations, Uprise Digital requires administrator or developer access to your content management system (CMS).

![[Placeholder Screenshot: CMS Administrator Access Panel]](/images/docs/aeo-access/cms-access-placeholder.svg)

### Option A: WordPress
1. Log into your WordPress Dashboard (`yourwebsite.com/wp-admin`).
2. Go to **Users** $\rightarrow$ **Add New User**.
3. Fill out the details:
   * **Username**: `uprisedigital`
   * **Email**: `ned@uprisedigital.com.au`
   * **Role**: Select **Administrator**.
4. Ensure **Send User Notification** is checked so our team receives the setup link.
5. Click **Add New User**.

### Option B: Shopify
1. Log into your Shopify Admin panel.
2. Navigate to **Settings** (bottom-left gear icon) $\rightarrow$ **Users and permissions**.
3. Under **Staff**, click **Add staff**.
4. Enter Name (*Ned - Uprise Digital*) and Email (**`ned@uprisedigital.com.au`**).
5. Grant full access permissions or select **Themes, Edit code, Navigation, Products, and Pages**.
6. Click **Send invite**.

### Option C: Webflow / Squarespace / Custom CMS
1. In your platform dashboard settings, navigate to **Collaborators**, **Team Members**, or **Account Permissions**.
2. Invite **`ned@uprisedigital.com.au`** as an **Admin** or **Developer**.
